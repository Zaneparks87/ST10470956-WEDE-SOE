Our Journey & Vision

Brief History

Founded in 2022 as a modest local lifestyle hyper store, ZaneparksHyper developed into a retail concept focused on quality everyday essentials. Our aim is to bring clothing and home essentials together in one convenient shopping experience.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is to offer a convenient shopping experience that includes quality clothing, everyday items, and home essentials that support modern everyday life.

Our Target Audience

We aim to serve busy families, active urban shoppers and people looking for a convenient place to explore clothing and home products without visiting multiple websites.
